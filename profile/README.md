# SoundBoardio

Free, installable soundboards powered by GitHub. Every board is a public repository with mp3 files and one `soundboardio.json` manifest; [soundboardio.com](https://soundboardio.com/en/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile) turns it into an app that works offline on your phone. No account, no build, no deploy.

**▶ [Browse the boards](https://soundboardio.com/en/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile)** · **[Make your own](https://soundboardio.com/create/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile)** (in Czech: [soundboardio.com](https://soundboardio.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile) · [guide](https://soundboardio.com/cs/create/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile))

## Your own board in three steps

1. Create a public repository whose name starts with `soundboardio-`, or press **Use this template** on [soundboardio-developers](https://github.com/soundboardio/soundboardio-developers).
2. Add your mp3 files and a `soundboardio.json`. The [generator](https://soundboardio.com/generator/) writes it for you and the [validator](https://soundboardio.com/validator/) checks it.
3. Open `soundboardio.com/app/<user>/<name>/`, where the name is your repository without the `soundboardio-` prefix. Changes in the repository show up on their own.

## Boards

The boards made here carry the [`soundboardio-plugin`](https://github.com/topics/soundboardio-plugin) topic. Every board the site lists, ours and the community's, is in [plugins](https://github.com/soundboardio/plugins).

Missing a quote on a board? Open an issue in its repository, the form asks for what is needed.
